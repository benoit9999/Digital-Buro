Before you visit.

Can you repair my device?

Call with the brand and model of your printer, PC or Mac. We check whether we can take it in and whether suitable parts are available.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the fault and the parts. Ask about diagnostic conditions before leaving your device. An estimate helps you decide whether to proceed.

Do I need an appointment?

Call 02 534 47 02 before visiting. We can explain how to bring your device in or arrange on-site support.

Do you stock ink and toner?

We carry cartridges, toners and ribbons, including references for older printers. Bring your old cartridge or its reference. Call to check availability.
