Frequently asked questions

Do you repair all brands?

Yes. HP, Canon, Epson, Brother, OKI, Xerox, Ricoh, Kyocera and Lexmark printers, HP, Dell, Lenovo, Acer and Asus computers, and Macs. We also service older machines such as dot matrix printers, fax machines and typewriters.

Do I need an appointment?

You can simply drop by the shop during opening hours: Monday to Friday from 10:30 to 18:00 and Saturday from 11:00 to 17:00. For on-site support, call us on 02 534 47 02.

Do you offer on-site support?

Yes, our technicians visit offices in Brussels, especially for office printers, copiers and networks.

Do you stock cartridges for older printers?

It is one of our specialities: we keep a large stock of cartridges, toner, drums and ribbons, including for models you will not find in big-box stores anymore.
